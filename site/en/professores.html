Judo & Jiu-Jitsu

Cid Nascimento

Cid began Judo at four, earned his Judo black belt at nineteen, founded his academy at twenty-four and earned his Jiu-Jitsu black belt at twenty-eight. He holds a degree in Physical Education.

Instruction languages: Portuguese, English and Spanish.

Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu

Juan Dias

A black belt under master Ricardinho (Checkmat), Juan is a competitive athlete with international experience. His achievements include a South American IBJJF title and medals in major Brazilian, South American and European events.

Instruction languages: Portuguese, English and Spanish.

Judo & Jiu-Jitsu

Ana

Ana began training at eleven and competed internationally from a young age, reaching the podium at a World Championship at fifteen. She has teaching experience in Brazil and abroad, and has earned medals at major IBJJF events.

Instruction languages: Portuguese, English and Spanish.

Boxing

Juan Joseph

A two-time state champion and Interstate Boxing Champion (cruiserweight). Record: 31 fights, 30 wins and 1 loss.

Judo & Jiu-Jitsu

Matheus Reis

Matheus joined Dojocid at twelve. The academy became an important place for personal growth and mentorship. Today he is a Judo black belt, a Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu brown belt and one of the academy's instructors.

Judo & Jiu-Jitsu

Lucas

Lucas started martial arts at six. Between ages nine and fourteen, he competed in more than twenty championships and won over nineteen gold medals. He is now an athlete, instructor and a Physiotherapy student nearing graduation.
