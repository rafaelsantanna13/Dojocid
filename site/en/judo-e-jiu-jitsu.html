Instructor

Cid Nascimento

Cid began practicing Judo at age four and trained at Universidade Gama Filho until he was eighteen. He earned his Judo black belt at nineteen and founded his own academy at twenty-four. He later earned a Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu black belt at twenty-eight with the Checkmat team. With a degree in Physical Education, he combines practical experience and technical knowledge in his teaching.

Instruction languages: Portuguese, English and Spanish.

For women

Self-defense, technique
and confidence.

Whether you are new to martial arts or already have experience, training can progress at your own pace. Ask our team about the right classes for you.

Judo: balance and control

Practice movement, balance, throws and body control, with a focus on using technique and positioning effectively.

Jiu-Jitsu: position and movement

Develop control, positioning, escapes and ground skills. Technique helps, but physical differences still require awareness and care.

Safety and awareness

Self-defense training also involves prevention, body awareness and decision-making. No technique guarantees safety in a real confrontation.

Confidence and fitness

Work on strength, endurance, mobility, coordination and focus while progressing at your own pace.
