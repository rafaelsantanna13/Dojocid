Instructor

Juan Joseph

A Boxe Para Todos athlete and instructor, Juan Joseph has practiced boxing since age seven. A two-time state champion and Interstate Boxing Champion in the cruiserweight division, he has fought 31 bouts, with 30 wins and just one loss. His background includes the Brazilian Boxing Association and training experiences alongside the Brazilian national team and leading boxers such as Acelino "Popó" Freitas and Olympic medalist Esquiva Falcão. At Dojocid, he shares his competitive and technical experience with students.
